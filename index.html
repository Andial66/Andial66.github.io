Santiago Cifuentes

Hi there! I am a theoretical computer scientist based in Buenos Aires. I am in the final year of my PhD at UBA, where I use ideas from theoretical computer science to approach foundational questions in quantum computing—mainly through computational complexity and Kolmogorov complexity. In recent years, I have also begun transitioning into AI safety, largely through a fellowship at Dovetail, where I work on agentic foundations.
